Method

Fewer voices. More evidence.

The prior orchestra crossed five methodologies with ten lenses and asked for fifty long opinions. On the July book, most of those voices were flagged thin. Quantity had become the cost.

  1. 01

    Sense

    One scout, with web search, returns a short evidence board across ten lenses. Empty lenses stay empty. Gaps are part of the record.

  2. 02

    Vote

    Five stateless workers run in parallel. Each sees the board and a role card. None sees another ballot.

  3. 03

    Score

    The published number is the median of the horizon the question actually asks. Spread is max minus min. The furthest ballot is named, not averaged away. That arithmetic does not spend a token.

  4. 04

    Speak

    A single writer turns the ballots into a sentence and falsifiable resolution criteria. It is instructed not to publish a different probability.

The five jobs

  • AnalystWeight official data and base rates. Distrust narrative. If the print is not in the board, say so.
  • SkepticName how the question could resolve no even if the story is right. Watch revisions, definitions, and timing.
  • QuantStart from what prices, spreads, vols, or betting markets already imply. If none are in the board, say the board is thin and stay near 50.
  • HistorianUse the closest precedent in the board. If there is no analogue, say so rather than inventing one.
  • ContrarianIdentify a neglected mechanism, not the reflexive opposite. Move far from 50 only when that mechanism is concrete in the evidence.

What scales

Workers are pure functions of the evidence board. They hold no memory of each other, so the fan-out can grow without a longer chain. The speaker always reads structured ballots, not transcripts.

Resolution is a separate search against criteria that were written first. A draft verdict stays off the public record until a person accepts it on the Desk. Nothing resolves itself.

Coverage is unchanged: macro, capital markets, crypto, geopolitics and policy, frontier AI and energy.
