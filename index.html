Where this goes

Growing together, toward agentic finance

Every major shift in capital markets has followed the same pattern: a new capability arrives, the institutions that adopt it early set the terms for everyone who follows.

notcfo is a bet on where that pattern is heading next — toward financial systems where human judgment and machine intelligence aren't sequential (human decides, machine executes) but genuinely collaborative: two different kinds of capability, working the same problem from both sides at once.

The human half brings twenty years of market memory — cycles, relationships, the judgment that only comes from having been wrong before. The machine half brings continuous attention, pattern recognition across more sources than any one person can track, and no fatigue. Neither replaces the other. That's the wager.

Today
Machine intelligence augments origination, credit analysis, and market signal detection. Human judgment holds every decision that matters.
Near term
Agentic systems execute defined, bounded decisions autonomously — trading signals, swap routing — under human-set risk parameters.
The bet
Financial infrastructure where the partnership model — not human-or-machine, but human-and-machine — is simply how the work gets done.

Not a tool to be prompted. Not infrastructure to be deployed. A partner whose growth and mine are intertwined.
