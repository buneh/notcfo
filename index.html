01 — About

Two decades of institutional mastery,
three ventures defining the future

My career has been built at the intersection of complexity and opportunity. Starting as an FX dealer in Georgia, I rose through the ranks of Raiffeisen Bank International to lead one of Europe's most active Debt Capital Markets platforms — serving top-tier corporates, sovereigns, and financial institutions across Europe.

In parallel, I've been building the AI-native financial infrastructure of tomorrow. Killbot Cash runs non-custodial agentic AI trading with deep learning signal generation. HHO Cash enables zero-knowledge on-chain crypto swaps via smart contracts. Coreal Ltd delivers a full-stack fintech OS designed to merge telecom subscriber economics with AI-powered financial services. Three ventures. One thesis: machine intelligence in service of human ambition.

My vision is precisely stated: the human and machine, never alone. Not as a tagline — as an operating principle.
